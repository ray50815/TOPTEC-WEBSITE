AREAS OF REVIEW

A transaction-specific approach.

The following areas may form part of a transaction’s review and execution arrangements. Applicability, responsibilities and the required documentation are determined for the individual engagement.

Counterparty due diligence

KYC / KYB information and beneficial ownership review may be requested according to the counterparty and transaction requirements.

Sanctions screening

Counterparty, vessel and transaction screening may be included according to the applicable requirements and agreed scope.

Cargo documentation

Relevant documents may include commercial invoices, bills of lading, quality and quantity certificates, inspection documentation and shipping records.

Independent inspection

Independent inspection may be coordinated with suitable inspection companies depending on the contract and transaction requirements.

Vessel & logistics review

Vessel documents, Q88 information, nomination requirements and terminal acceptance procedures may be reviewed where applicable.
