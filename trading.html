OUR APPROACH

A connected transaction workflow.

We coordinate with suppliers, buyers, terminals, inspection companies and shipping partners as required. Each transaction defines its own commercial terms, responsibilities and operational requirements.

  1. 01

    Commercial requirements

    Align the product, quantity, destination and delivery requirements.

  2. 02

    Contract & compliance review

    Review counterparties, commercial terms and relevant transaction requirements.

  3. 03

    Cargo arrangements

    Coordinate cargo nomination and the agreed operational milestones.

  4. 04

    Inspection & documentation

    Coordinate applicable inspection arrangements and cargo documentation.

  5. 05

    Marine & terminal coordination

    Align transport, terminal acceptance and storage arrangements where required.

  6. 06

    Delivery coordination

    Follow the agreed delivery arrangements and documentation handover.

An indicative workflow. The sequence and applicable steps depend on each transaction.

AGREED FOR EACH TRANSACTION

Clear scope.
Documented handovers.

Product specification, inspection requirements, documentation, timing and delivery responsibilities are discussed during commercial review and recorded in the relevant agreements.

Storage or marine transport may be involved according to the delivery structure. The illustrative workflow does not imply that every transaction uses the same facilities or sequence.

TRANSACTION STANDARDS

Standard checks,
built into each trade.

Depending on the counterparties and cargo, a transaction may include:

  • Counterparty due diligence
  • Sanctions screening of parties and vessels
  • Vessel documentation and terminal acceptance
  • Independent inspection and cargo documentation
