ABOUT TOPTEC

A Singapore trading company.
A clear energy focus.

Commercial sourcing and physical cargo coordination for refined petroleum products.

OUR BUSINESS

Energy trading,
with practical coordination.

TOPTEC GLOBAL PTE. LTD. is a Singapore-based trading company established in 2019, engaged in the wholesale and international trade of fuels and related petroleum products.

Our activities cover commercial sourcing, cargo coordination, storage and terminal arrangements, marine logistics, documentation and transaction-specific compliance coordination. We work with suppliers, buyers, terminal operators, inspection companies and logistics partners as required for each transaction.

TOPTEC has historically been involved in international wholesale and electronics-related trading and has since expanded its principal business into energy and refined petroleum products.

HOW WE WORK

Third-party infrastructure.
Transaction-specific arrangements.

Physical delivery is coordinated with third-party infrastructure and service providers. The roles, scope and responsibilities of each party are set out in the relevant transaction agreements.
